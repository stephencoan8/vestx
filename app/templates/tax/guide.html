Sources of truth

  1. IRS / SSA / FTB / EDD published tables for the tax year — brackets, standard deductions, SS wage base, NIIT, AMT, harbors.
  2. Your paystub for YTD federal and CA withholding when you enter it. VestX does not guess stub YTD.
  3. Shareworks / the broker tape for share counts. If VestX and Shareworks disagree by a few shares, believe Shareworks until a reconcile pass.
  4. VestX lots and sales for character (RSU vs ESPP vs ISO) and for recorded exercises.

Peer tools (Holistiplan, Carta, ESO) use different wage and gain inputs. A $1–2k gap against them is usually input drift, not a reason to change VestX totals.

RSUs

At vest, FMV × shares is W-2 wages (Box 1) and FICA. Supplemental withholding on the stub is typically 22% federal and 10.23% CA — that is withholding, not the tax. Basis for a later sale is that vest FMV. Holding ≥ 1 year from vest is long-term federally; California does not care (gains are ordinary PIT either way).

ESPP — IRC §423

This is not an RSU. Treating a sale as “LTCG vs FMV basis, ordinary $0” is the wrong statute. VestX uses a single §423 engine for the planner, optimizer, recorded sales, and the year stack.

Purchase price (lookback)

You pay (1 − discount) × min(FMV at offering start, FMV at purchase). Statutory discount cap is 15%. Example: grant/offering FMV $50, purchase FMV $80, 15% → you paid $42.50.

The two-year qualifying clock starts at the beginning of the offering period, not the purchase date. If VestX only stored a purchase date, offering start is inferred as six months earlier (standard SpaceX-style offering). You can store an explicit offering start on the grant when you have it.

Qualifying disposition (QD)

Hold ≥ 2 years from offering start and ≥ 1 year from purchase. Ordinary income is the lesser of (grant-date FMV − purchase price) and actual gain. In the example that is $7.50/sh, not the $37.50 purchase bargain. The rest is capital gain. Long-term if held ≥ 1 year from purchase.

Disqualifying disposition (DD)

Anything that is not QD.

  • Sale at $100: ordinary = purchase FMV − purchase price = $37.50/sh; residual CG = $20/sh (sale − purchase FMV).
  • Sale at $60 (between $42.50 and $80): ordinary is capped at actual gain ($17.50); CG $0.
  • Sale below $42.50: ordinary $0; capital loss vs purchase price.

Statutory §423 ordinary is compensation for income tax and not FICA. Non-qualified ESPP (NQESPP) is wages for FICA.

The $25,000 annual limit is FMV at grant × shares in the offering. VestX flags an excess; it does not silently split the lot into statutory vs non-statutory.

ISOs, AMT, qualifying vs disqualifying

Vest: nothing on the 1040 and no AMT. Unexercised ISO vests must never create AMT due. A planning-only bargain figure may show so you can see what AMT would look like if you exercised — it does not hit TOTAL TAX, Expected tax, April, or extra W-4.

Exercise and hold: regular tax unchanged. AMT preference = FMV at exercise − strike. Federal TMT is 26%/28% after the 2026 exemption ($90,100 single, phaseout starting $500,000 at 50%). Long-term capital gain inside AMTI is taxed at preferential 0/15/20% (Form 6251-style), not 26/28%, so a large LTCG stack does not fake AMT. California Schedule P–style AMT is 7% after CA exemption; the ISO planner prints Fed AMT and CA AMT as separate dollars, including $0.

Same-year cashless / DD: bargain is ordinary wages (and FICA). No ISO AMT preference on those shares.

Qualifying sale: 2 years from grant and 1 year from exercise. Entire gain over strike is long-term capital gain federally. AMT was the exercise-year problem; this year may use AMT credit entered on Tax profile.

Federal, California, payroll

  • Federal ordinary: 2026 brackets after $16,100 standard deduction (single), unless you itemize above that (SALT capped).
  • LTCG/QD bands 0/15/20% stacked on taxable ordinary.
  • NIIT 3.8% on the lesser of net investment income and MAGI over $200,000 (single). Compensation is not NII; sale CG is.
  • CA PIT uses FTB 2026 540-ES standard deduction $5,706 (single) and 2025 Schedule X as the planning proxy — final 2026 Schedule X is not published. Gains are ordinary. Mental Health Services Tax 1% over $1M taxable income.
  • FICA: SS 6.2% up to $184,500 (max $11,439); Medicare 1.45%; Additional Medicare 0.9% over $200k. Full-year W-2 never uses “SS already maxed.”
  • Payroll on this account uses employer VPDI 1.1% from the SpaceX stub, not statutory SDI 1.3%.
  • Net capital loss offsets ordinary, capped at $3,000 ($1,500 MFS) under IRC §1211.

Withholding, April, extra W-4, safe harbor

These are four different numbers. Cloning them is a cash-planning bug.

  • Locked-in withholding — stub YTD federal $42,110.89 and CA $19,257.25 when entered; otherwise a model.
  • Expected withholding — locked-in plus remaining paycheck/vest supplemental.
  • Underpaid / April balance / tax to set aside — Expected tax minus expected withholding. Sales tax + vest true-up should foot to this.
  • Extra W-4 on remaining RSUs — incremental income tax on remaining RSU vests minus already-modeled supplemental. Not the April bill, not sales tax, not AMT. You cannot withhold extra on ESPP or unexercised ISOs through payroll the same way.
  • Safe harbor — high-AGI filer: 110% of prior-year total tax or 90% of current-year tax; VestX takes the lower required annual. 2025 year-tax ~$80,685 → 110% ~$88,753. Both tests are legal; the UI must show both. CA 540-ES installments are 30% / 40% / 0% / 30%.

Profile locked-in sale gain is the Activity ledger ST+LT total, not leftover “other LTCG” on the profile form.

Ask Grok vs the optimizer

Optimize lots on Plan is the tax engine: SpecID picks, dollars, no narrative. That is intentional.

Ask Grok (the button on every page, and “Ask Grok about this plan”) always calls Grok. If the question is computable — net cash, ISO QD dates, year income — the engine still runs first and Grok has to use those dollars. It should not answer “how does ESPP disqualifying work?” with a lot-picker dump.

What this engine still does not do

Planning-grade means these are out until they are built. Do not assume they are hiding in a total.

  • Full Form 6251 Part III (incentive stock, depletion, etc. beyond ISO bargain + LTCG split).
  • Final FTB 2026 Schedule X and 2026 CA Schedule P exemption (we use 2025 X and an inflated 2025 P exemption).
  • Qualified dividends, interest, tax-exempt interest add-back to NIIT MAGI — enter as other ST/LT if you must, they are not first-class fields yet.
  • Wash sales, 83(b), NQSO/NSO as their own share type, QBI, 401(k) deferral, multiple-employer FICA.
  • Actual §6654 underpayment penalty dollars — we test harbor vs YTD credits only.
  • Splitting ESPP lots that exceed the $25k offering cap into statutory vs non-statutory.
  • Shareworks share-count drift (on the order of ~7 held / 10 ISO / 11 next vest). Display conflict; do not silently “fix” Shareworks.
  • States other than California: flat rate if you set one, not a full state engine.
